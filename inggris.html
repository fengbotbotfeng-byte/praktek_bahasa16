BOLAANG MONGONDOW

Bolaang Mongondow · North Sulawesi
About This Story

Origin & Setting

A local folktale telling how the name Bolaang Mongondow is connected to the land, hope, and kinship.

OriginBolaang Mongondow, North Sulawesi
SettingThe mountains and land around Mount Komasan
2
Chapter One

The Beginning of Life in the Inland

In ancient times, in a mountainous area and dense forest in the land now known as Bolaang Mongondow, there lived a husband and wife named Abo' Tadohe and Mole'bu. They lived side by side with nature, depending for their livelihood on the produce of the forest and the rivers around their dwelling place.

Their days passed peacefully until one time nature showed extraordinary turmoil. Heavy rain fell continuously for days, triggering flash floods and a very large flood. The water current overflowed quickly, submerging the valleys and inundating almost all the land they usually stepped on.

3
Chapter Two

Surviving on Mount Komasan

In the midst of panic due to the flood water that kept rising, Abo' Tadohe and Mole'bu tried to save themselves. They grabbed a large, sturdy log and held on tightly as the swift current swept them away from their old dwelling place.

Their struggle was not in vain. The log carried them until they were stranded on the peak of a high mountain known as Mount Komasan. At that height they survived while waiting for the water to recede, strengthening each other.

4
Chapter Three

The Brightness of “Bolaang”

After some time passed, the flood water slowly receded. From the peak of Mount Komasan, Abo' Tadohe and Mole'bu looked down and saw a vast expanse of land that was starting to dry and looked bright and clear, struck by sunlight after previously being covered by water and fog.

In the Mongondow language, the view of the land that is bright and wide open is called “Bolaang.” For Abo' Tadohe and Mole'bu, the view became a sign of new hope to start life again and arrange a settlement in a safer and more fertile area.

5
Chapter Four

The Call of “Momondow”

As time went by, their descendants grew in number. Their children and grandchildren began to spread in various directions, both to coastal areas and to inland areas, to open land and build new lives.

In order for the kinship relationship to remain maintained even though distance separated them, they called out to each other and gave news with the call “Momondow!”, which means to call out or to give a sign to a distant brother or sister to gather and strengthen each other.

6
Chapter Five

The Birth of the Name Bolaang Mongondow

From the combination of two meaningful words in the local language, the name Bolaang Mongondow was born:

BolaangA vast area that is bright, open, and full of hope after a difficult period.
MongondowThe spirit of brotherhood of the community who call out to each other, greet each other, and unite in togetherness.

Since then, the story of the perseverance of Abo' Tadohe and Mole'bu has continued to be remembered as the root of the history and cultural identity of the Bolaang Mongondow community.
